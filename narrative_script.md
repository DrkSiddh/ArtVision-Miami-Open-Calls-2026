# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment: Miami Art Week Free Exhibit Opportunities

*Conversational coffee-chat style – encouraging, uplifting, authoritative. For the ArtVision brand.*

---

Hey, so I’ve been out there scraping the internet, X, Instagram, all the usual places for free ways to get your art shown during Miami Art Week this year. Grab your coffee, sit down – I’ve got a couple of really solid ones that are still open, no cost to you, and they include actual exhibition during the week. Miami Art Week runs roughly November 30 to December 6, 2026, right around Art Basel Miami Beach.

First up, and this one’s perfect if you’re into digital or that Web3/crypto rendering side of things: the Render Network’s Miami After Dark open call. They’re selecting 10 to 15 artists for a big 50-foot large-scale projection exhibition on Miami Beach on December 2nd. Theme is “Miami After Dark,” so think night vibes, energy, that Miami glow. Your piece needs to be up to about 2 minutes looping animation, and it has to be rendered on their Render Network – but they cover the rendering costs with credits for the selected artists. Zero application fee. It’s open, and the Typeform is quick – takes about 7 minutes. They’re looking for original digital work that fits the theme. Deadline’s been listed variously as end of October or mid-November depending on the source, so check the form ASAP because spots are limited and it’s closing soon. Google “Render Network Miami After Dark open call” or go straight to the Typeform: https://renderfoundation.typeform.com/to/NGgLRjhY. On X they’re @rendernetwork, and they’ve posted about it on Instagram too. This gets you exhibited at architectural scale during the biggest art week in the US, plus that tech/Web3 story with decentralized GPU rendering. Super cool visibility.

Next, and this is huge prestige with zero barrier: the inaugural .ART Award from the .ART Registry, powered with HUG. No traditional application form, no fees. Your .ART domain is your application – point it to your website, portfolio, HUG profile, Instagram, or even OpenSea, as long as it shows your work, a short bio (around 100 words), and the process/story behind the art. Free .ART domains are available through HUG for a year if you don’t have one yet. Deadline is November 1, 2026. Winners get announced right at Art Basel Miami Beach on December 3rd. Prizes total over $50,000 – $15k cash grand prize, residencies in France and Spain, Whitewall Magazine feature, premium domain, and more. Jury includes Jerry Saltz, Sasha Stiles, and other big names from the art world. All disciplines, global, 18+. There’s also an opt-in to have work shown in a gallery at BitBasel. Super encouraging for digital, process-focused, and Web3-adjacent artists. Details at https://www.award.art/ or https://hug.art/open-calls/ARTaward. Google “.ART Award 2026 Miami” or “HUG .ART Award”.

These are the kind of opportunities that can really open doors without costing you anything. We’re building this into a running, searchable database so each day we can surface 1-3 new ones, and people can search for MIAMI plus keywords and only see what’s still open with their deadlines and URLs. You’ve got this – put your best work out there and go claim some of that Miami spotlight.
