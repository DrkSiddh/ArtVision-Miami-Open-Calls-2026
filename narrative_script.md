# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment: Miami Art Week Exhibition Opportunities

*Casual but authoritative \u2014 like a friend filling you in over coffee at the caf\u00e9. Encouraging, uplifting, never punching down.*

Hey, grab a seat. Miami Art Week is right around the corner \u2014 November 30 through December 6, 2026, with Art Basel Miami Beach smack in the middle. The whole city becomes this electric art playground, and the beautiful part is there are still real doors open for artists to get work exhibited without paying a fortune.

I\u2019ve been hunting the free ones, especially anything digital or Web3-flavored, because that\u2019s where so much of the fresh energy lives right now. Clear deadlines, no (or very justified) fees, and actual exhibition plus entry to the event. Here\u2019s what\u2019s still open and solid as of today, October 29.

First, the one that feels made for us: **Render Network\u2019s Miami After Dark**.

They\u2019re selecting 10 to 15 artists for a 50-foot large-scale projection on Miami Beach, at The National Hotel, on December 2, 2026. Theme is \u201cMiami After Dark.\u201d Your piece needs to be rendered on the Render Network \u2014 that decentralized GPU compute network, pure Web3-adjacent artist support \u2014 up to two minutes long, and they cover the rendering credits for whoever is selected. Zero application fee.

What they\u2019re looking for: original digital work with real visual punch at architectural scale. The application is a short Typeform that takes about 7 minutes. Roughly 6 to 9 questions covering your contact info, a short concept description, and the submission itself. Receipts are light: the rendered piece (or strong portfolio examples + concept), links to your site or Instagram, name/email, and a quick why-this-showcase note.

Deadlines are listed differently across sources \u2014 some say October 30, others November 2 or as late as November 15 \u2014 but the form is live right now, so apply today. Google \u201cRender Network Miami After Dark\u201d or go straight here: https://renderfoundation.typeform.com/to/NGgLRjhY

You get your work projected outdoors during the week the entire art world is already in town. Entry to the activation comes with being selected. This is the kind of visibility that can open bigger doors, and it\u2019s completely free. Super encouraging if you\u2019re working digitally.

Next, a global prestige play that lands right in Miami: the **.ART Award 2026**.

Entry is free. You submit a .ART domain URL that points to wherever your work already lives \u2014 your site, Hug profile, OpenSea, Instagram, whatever. The domain itself is usually cheap for the first year (often a few dollars or free through partners), and the submission costs nothing. Winners are announced on December 3, 2026, during Art Basel Miami Beach. Jury includes heavyweights like Jerry Saltz. Over $50,000 in prizes: $15k cash, residencies in France, Spain, and Georgia, Whitewall editorial, a premium .ART domain, a Tezos prize for on-chain artists, Times Square screens later, and more. You can even opt in for screens at BitBasel in Miami.

What they\u2019re looking for is the story behind the work as much as the finished piece. Requirements are three clear receipts: a bio of at least 100 words, high-quality images/video/3D of the work with technical details, and either a 100+ word written narrative or a 30-second to 1-minute video of you talking about the process. No traditional application questions \u2014 your URL *is* the application.

Deadline: November 1, 2026, at 12:00 UTC. Details at https://www.award.art/ \u2014 just Google \u201c.ART Award 2026.\u201d

Why the small domain cost is worth it: international prize with real cash, real jury, and a public announcement during the biggest week of the American art calendar. Plus you walk away with a permanent digital home for your practice. Uplifting as hell if you\u2019ve been building online.

And if you want something ongoing and easy: **Art Miami Magazine\u2019s Open Call for Artist Interviews**. Free, rolling, email submission. Send 6 high-res photos, a 200\u2013400 word statement, short bio, and your links. They consider selected artists for special print editions distributed during Miami Art Week. No form, just solid editorial visibility. Details at https://artmiamimagazine.com/open-call-for-artist-interviews/

Those are the strongest free (or essentially free) ones still accepting that actually get your work exhibited or celebrated during Miami Art Week, with a clear digital/Web3 lean.

A few others have already closed (Art Miami Magazine print edition was October 20, SaveArtSpace bus shelters was October 19 and had a fee). Physical gallery spots with the official Miami Art Week programs usually carry fees. Fridge Art Fair is open until October 31 with a $35 per work fee that funds their independent South Beach venues \u2014 fun and inclusive if that budget works for you.

You\u2019ve got this. The work is already happening \u2014 these are just more places for it to be seen. Go apply, then tell me how it goes. More coffee next time.
