# ArtVision Miami Art Week 2026 Open Calls – Podcast Script
## Special Segment: Friend Over Coffee at the Café

Hey, so grab your coffee — I just finished scanning for real, no-cost ways to get your digital or physical work in front of people during Miami Art Week this year. That’s the week around November 30 to December 6, 2026, with Art Basel Miami Beach right in the middle. I zeroed in on clear deadlines, free entry for the artist, and especially the Web3 or crypto-adjacent ones. Voice of a friend who’s rooting for you: encouraging, uplifting, zero punching down. These are genuine chances to exhibit.

First one that jumped out: Render Network’s Miami After Dark. They’re picking 10 to 15 artists for a huge 50-foot projection on Miami Beach, at The National Hotel, on December 2, 2026. Theme is literally “Miami After Dark” — that after-hours energy, neon, nightlife, the city when the fairs wind down. Your piece needs to be up to two minutes long and rendered on the Render Network, but they give you the credits so the technical side is covered. No application fee. Open to U.S. artists for digital work. The Typeform is about nine questions and takes roughly seven minutes. You’ll share your name, email, artist name, a portfolio link or Drive folder of previous work, and details about the proposed piece. Deadline is November 15, 2026. Apply right here: https://renderfoundation.typeform.com/to/NGgLRjhY. Google “Render Network Miami After Dark open call” or check their X @rendernetwork. This is pure scale and exposure during the biggest art week, with that decentralized GPU / Web3 infrastructure angle. If your work lives on screen, this is a beautiful stage.

Next, from the heart of the crypto art community: One Love Art DAO’s FRONTERA open call. They want work that challenges how we see things and shifts perspectives. Selected pieces go up in their gallery at Hotel Yve in downtown Miami during Art Week, and you can be considered for extra partner spots. Deadline is also November 15, 2026. Completely free — they confirmed it on X, open to global artists, digital or physical, minted or not, any chain. The form is https://forms.gle/DjERA61QPdZEf19SA (Google Form, may ask you to sign in). Expect a handful of fields: contact info, a short statement on how the work fits FRONTERA, links to portfolio or marketplace, and the artwork itself (image or short video). They’re a nonprofit 501(c)(3) with a permanent digital gallery presence. Search “One Love Art DAO FRONTERA” or @OneLoveArtDAO. Community-driven, welcoming, and exactly the kind of Web3 space that lifts independent artists.

Those two are the free, clear-deadline, entry-included opportunities that are still open right now. Always double-check the form the day you apply because dates can shift. Your work deserves to be seen in Miami — go submit while the window is open. What are you making? I can help you match the right one.
