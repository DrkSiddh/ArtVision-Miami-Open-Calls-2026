# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment: Miami Art Week Exhibition Opportunities

*Casual but authoritative — like a friend filling you in over coffee at the café. Encouraging, uplifting, never punching down.*

Hey, grab a seat. Miami Art Week is coming up fast — November 30 through December 6, 2026, with Art Basel Miami Beach right in the middle of it. The whole city turns into this massive, buzzing art playground, and the good news is there are real, open doors for artists like us to get work on the walls (or the giant projections, or the screens) without dropping a fortune.

I’ve been digging for the free ones, especially anything digital or web3-flavored, because that’s where the energy is right now. Clear deadlines, no (or very justified) fees, and actual exhibition — not just “maybe we’ll consider it.” Here are the ones still open that feel solid.

First up, and this one is pure web3 magic: **Render Network’s Miami After Dark**.

They’re picking 10 to 15 artists for a 50-foot large-scale projection on Miami Beach, at The National Hotel, on December 2, 2026. Theme is “Miami After Dark.” Your piece has to be rendered on the Render Network (that’s the decentralized GPU thing — crypto-adjacent, artist-friendly compute), up to two minutes long, and they cover the rendering credits for selected artists. No application fee. Open to digital and visual artists; some listings say U.S. only, so double-check, but the form is live.

What they’re looking for: original work that fits the night vibe, strong visual impact at scale. Application is a Typeform with **9 questions**. Receipts: your portfolio website or Google Drive link with examples of previous work, Instagram or other social handle, a short description of your submission idea, plus the usual name/email/artist name and a why-this-showcase note. Pretty light lift.

Deadline: sources vary between October 30 and November 15, 2026 — the form is open right now, so apply sooner rather than later. Google “Render Network Miami After Dark” or go straight to the Typeform: https://renderfoundation.typeform.com/to/NGgLRjhY

You get exhibited on that big outdoor projection during the week everyone is already in town. Entry to the activation is part of being selected. Super encouraging for anyone working digitally — this is the kind of visibility that can open other doors.

Next, a more global, prestige play that still lands in Miami: the **.ART Award 2026**.

This is free to enter. You submit a .ART domain URL that points to your site, Hug profile, OpenSea, Instagram, whatever already holds your work. The domain itself is cheap for the first year (often under $5–10), and the submission itself costs nothing. Winners are announced on December 3, 2026, during Art Basel Miami Beach. Jury includes heavy hitters like Jerry Saltz. Over $50,000 in prizes: $15k cash grand prize, residencies in France, Spain, Georgia, editorial in Whitewall, a premium .ART domain, Tezos prize for on-chain artists, Times Square screens later, AR grant… the works. You can even opt in to have work shown on screens at BitBasel in Miami.

What they’re looking for: the story behind the work as much as the work itself. Requirements are three clear pieces of material: a bio of at least 100 words, high-quality images/video/3D of the work with tech details, and either a 100+ word written narrative or a 30-second to 1-minute video of you talking about the process. No traditional application questions — your URL *is* the application.

Deadline: November 1, 2026, 12:00 UTC. Details and form at https://www.award.art/ — just Google “.ART Award 2026.”

Why the small domain cost is worth it: it’s an international prize with real cash, real jury, and a public announcement during the biggest week of the American art calendar. Plus you walk away with a permanent digital home for your practice. Uplifting as hell if you’ve been building online.

Those two are the strongest free (or essentially free) ones still accepting right now that actually get your work exhibited or celebrated during Miami Art Week, with a clear web3/digital lean.

A couple of others have already closed (Art Miami Magazine print edition was October 20, SaveArtSpace bus shelters was October 19 and had a fee). Physical gallery spots with Miami Art Week itself usually carry fees and earlier deadlines. Vizcaya has a free open call for a South Florida residency + commission ending November 2, but that’s for 2027 exhibition, not this week — still worth knowing if you’re local: vizcaya.org/opencall/

You’ve got this. The work is already happening — these are just more places for it to be seen. Go apply, then tell me how it goes. More coffee next time.
