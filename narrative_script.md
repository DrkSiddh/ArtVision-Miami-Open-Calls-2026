# ArtVision Miami Art Week 2026 Open Calls – Podcast Script
## Special Segment: Friend Over Coffee at the Café

Hey, so I was digging around for real opportunities to get your work out during Miami Art Week this year – you know, the big one around early December, Art Basel Miami Beach and all the satellites. I focused on stuff that's free or has a clear, good reason for any fee, especially digital, physical, or Web3/crypto related. Clear deadlines, entry included where it makes sense, and no punching down – these are solid chances to shine.

First up, the one that feels perfect if you're into digital and tech: Render Network's Miami After Dark activation. They're selecting 10 to 15 artists for a massive 50-foot projection on Miami Beach, at The National Hotel, on December 2, 2026. Theme is "Miami After Dark" – think nightlife energy, that electric Miami vibe after the sun goes down. Works up to 2 minutes long, and they have to be rendered on the Render Network, but they cover the rendering costs with credits, so it's not a barrier. No application fee at all. It's open to artists of any nationality for digital and visual arts. The Typeform is quick, about 9 questions, takes around 7 minutes. You'll need your name, email, and likely some portfolio links or examples of your style, plus a concept description. Deadline seems tight – some listings say October 30 or November 2, others November 15, so check the form ASAP: https://renderfoundation.typeform.com/to/NGgLRjhY. Google "Render Network Miami After Dark open call" for updates. This is pure exposure during the busiest art week, with that Web3 decentralized GPU angle. Super encouraging if your work thrives on screen and scale.

Next, from the Web3 side, One Love Art DAO just dropped an open call for their FRONTERA theme. They're looking for works that challenge our understanding and shift perspectives. Selected artists get exhibited at their art gallery at Hotel Yve in Miami during Art Week, and considered for additional partner opportunities. Deadline is November 15, 2026. It's free – no fee mentioned. The form is a Google Form: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdqPVMlUMsAbZOW3v48Z8tacY-nSzYWsSUckDA_2ryFQ17H3g/viewform (might need a Google login). From what I saw, people are uploading videos or images, so expect a few portfolio pieces, a statement or description of how it fits FRONTERA, and your bio or links. They have a permanent digital gallery vibe and are a nonprofit 501(c)(3) collective. Search "One Love Art DAO FRONTERA" or check their X @OneLoveArtDAO. This feels community-driven and uplifting for crypto art folks.

If you're okay with a modest fee for something prestigious, Nova Gallery is running a Miami Art Week 2026 online exhibition presented through Artsy – one of the top platforms for discovering and selling art. Deadline November 20, 2026. Exhibition runs December 3, 2026 to January 5, 2027. $40 application fee, which gets you considered for the online show during Art Basel, and selected artists may be considered for 12-month non-exclusive representation. Up to 5 images of your work, plus artist statement. All mediums welcome, including digital and new media. Apply via ArtConnect: https://www.artconnect.com/opportunity/gOFrOmD67OnaUQ_VOTjrH. The fee is justified by the Artsy visibility and potential gallery path – not just another pay-to-play. Google "Nova Gallery Miami Art Week Artsy".

Finally, SaveArtSpace has "SO THEY SAY", a public art exhibition on bus shelter ad spaces in Miami, opening December 30, 2026 (right after Art Week, still in the glow). Curated by Katelyn Kopenhaver. Theme is about advertising everywhere and questioning what we're being sold – humor or critical inquiry welcome. Deadline November 23, 2026. Fee is $35 for 1-3 images (tax-deductible donation supporting the ad space production), +$10 per extra. Selected announced after December 7, on view at least a month. Apply: https://form.jotform.com/saveartspace/so-they-say-upload-form or see https://www.saveartspace.org/so-they-say. Public exposure like this is rare and powerful – your work interrupting the commercial noise. Google "SaveArtSpace SO THEY SAY Miami".
